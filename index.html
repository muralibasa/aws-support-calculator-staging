2026 Plans
⚠️
Legacy Plans End of Support — January 1, 2027. Developer Support, Business Support, and Enterprise On-Ramp are being discontinued. Enterprise On-Ramp customers will be auto-upgraded to Enterprise Support during 2026 renewal cycles.
🤝
Partner-Led Support (PLS) is billed to AWS Solution Provider Partners, not end customers directly. Partners resell support to their customers. PLS pricing is the same tier structure as Enterprise Support but with a $7,500/month minimum. Partners may also qualify for discounts up to 40% based on quality and performance metrics.
